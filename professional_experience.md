## 1. Unified Payments Limited
*   **Position:** IT Intern (SIWES) — PTSA Operations
*   **Duration:** July 2026 – Present

### Professional Summary
At Unified Payments, I gained practical exposure to enterprise payment infrastructure, financial technology operations, and transaction processing systems. My role provided insight into how large-scale payment ecosystems operate behind the scenes, strengthening my understanding of fintech architecture, payment terminal management, enterprise databases, and operational support.

Working within the PTSA Operations team allowed me to bridge classroom knowledge with production environments, exposing me to the reliability, precision, and security requirements of financial systems that process transactions at national scale.

### Responsibilities
*   Supported daily PTSA operational activities.
*   Assisted with payment terminal provisioning and management.
*   Worked with enterprise SQL databases.
*   Performed data validation and transaction-related investigations.
*   Used SQL to query operational datasets.
*   Assisted in troubleshooting payment infrastructure issues.
*   Supported reconciliation and operational reporting.
*   Worked with enterprise collaboration tools.
*   Documented operational processes and technical observations.
*   Participated in technical discussions around payment systems.

### Technology Stack
*   **Databases:** PostgreSQL, SQL
*   **Tools:** DBeaver, DataGrip, Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat
*   **Knowledge Areas:** POS Infrastructure, Transaction Processing, Payment Operations, Enterprise Systems, Financial Technology, Operational Support

### Professional Growth
This internship significantly expanded my understanding of enterprise software beyond application development. It demonstrated how mission-critical systems prioritize reliability, consistency, security, and operational excellence over rapid feature delivery. Working within a fintech environment also reinforced the importance of structured processes, database accuracy, and collaboration across technical and operational teams.

### Skills & Competencies Developed
*   **Technical:** SQL querying, PostgreSQL, Database exploration, Operational troubleshooting, Enterprise software workflows
*   **Professional:** Documentation, Cross-functional collaboration, Operational discipline, Technical communication, Analytical thinking

### Lessons Learned
*   Enterprise software emphasizes stability over novelty.
*   Financial systems require rigorous attention to accuracy.
*   Operational teams are essential to product reliability.
*   Databases underpin every transaction.
*   Small errors can have significant downstream impact.
